# RAVE ROLLERS: Lore Bible

> *"Nobody's ever heard THE SET. Everybody knows someone who almost did."*

This document is the source of truth for the world, its people, and its voice. The data files in `src/data/` are written to match it.

---

## 1. Premise

It is the **last night of the Neon Nocturne**, a three-day festival in a dry valley. It runs on wristbands, glowsticks and bad phone signal. Somewhere around the second encore, **you lost your crew**. Your phone is at 2%, and the group chat is a wall of half-delivered messages.

People say your favourite DJ, **Halcyon Ray**, plays a secret sunrise set on the last morning, unannounced and unlisted. On the forums it's just called **THE SET**. Nobody has a recording. Everyone has a story.

So you walk deeper into the festival to find your friends, to find Halcyon Ray, and to be there when the sun comes up.

The night isn't playing fair, though. Something is draining the vibe out of the crowd. Ravers stand frozen mid-step with their glowsticks gone grey, then shamble toward anyone still dancing. They're called **the Drained**. The deeper you go, the stranger the night gets.

**Core rule of the world:** THE SET is never shown. The player can reach the Sunrise Set stage and keep going into Afterhours. The sun is always *about* to come up.

## 2. Tone

- **Warm, funny, and a bit melancholy.** It's a festival story about friendship and wanting to belong to a moment.
- **Surreal, not seedy.** The trippiness lives only in the visuals and the writing: melting stages, a disco-ball moon, a totem that talks. **There are no drugs, drug references, or winks at real substances anywhere in the game.** Power-ups are festival magic: glowsticks, electrolytes, kandi, second winds and bass face.
- **Every name is original.** No real DJs, artists, labels, or festivals.
- **Kind to the player.** Crashing out isn't death. You wake up in the Medical Tent or Back Home with a foil blanket and a cup of water, and you try again.

## 3. Core Terms

| In-world term | What it is | Mechanic |
|---|---|---|
| **Energy** | How long you can keep going | Hit points. 0 Energy = you **crash**. |
| **Vibe** | Momentum on the floor | Skill resource. Built by **Dancing** (basic attack) and **Catching the Beat** (Defend). |
| **Hydration** | Water, coconut water, electrolytes | Small, free heals that cleanse minor debuffs. Refilled at **Water Stations**. |
| **Food** | Festival food-truck meals | Big heal plus a fight-long buff, but eating **costs your turn**. |
| **Glowsticks** | Festival currency (yes, really) | Gold. |
| **Afterglow** | What you carry home from every night | Meta currency (unlocks, perks). |
| **The Drained** | Vibe-sucked zombie ravers | The basic enemies. |
| **THE SET** | Halcyon Ray's rumoured sunrise set | The thing you chase. Never seen. |

### The Genre Wheel
Four sounds, each loud enough to drown out the next:

**Bass > Tempo > Wall > Flow > Bass**

- **Bass** (Dubstep): wobble, weight, the drop. Applies **Feedback** (damage over time).
- **Tempo** (Drum & Bass): speed, multi-hits, crits. Applies **Drain** (decaying damage).
- **Wall** (Hardstyle): kicks, guard, stun. Builds **Guard** (shield).
- **Flow** (House): groove, healing, endurance. Applies **Muffled** (slows).

Hitting the genre your sound beats does 1.5x damage. Hitting the one that beats you does 0.75x.

### Status Glossary
- **Feedback:** takes N damage at turn start, then N-1.
- **Drain:** takes N damage at turn start; stacks decay.
- **Muffled:** -30% speed.
- **Dazzled:** skips its turn.
- **Guard:** absorbs damage; halves each turn.
- **Hype:** +30% damage.
- **Tired:** -25% damage.
- **Spikes:** attackers take damage back.

## 4. The Wanderers (Playable Classes)

### Bass Brawler (Dubstep)
A magenta-mohawked heavyweight with a subwoofer gauntlet strapped to one fist. Came for the drop, stayed for the friends. Talks loud and hugs louder.
- **Passive, Rattle:** hits apply Feedback.
- **Evolutions:** Subwoofer Scrapper → Bass Brawler → Earthquake Headliner.

### Flow Healer (House)
A cyan-haired hooper whose LED hoop leaves halos in the air. Knows where every water station is. Keeps the crew alive, usually by force.
- **Passive, Groove:** recovers Energy every turn.
- **Evolutions:** Hula Hooper → Flow Healer → Four-on-the-Floor Oracle.

### Tempo Dancer (Drum & Bass)
Lime-green buns, a tracksuit, and a glowstick in each hand. Already moving before the beat drops. Has never once stood still in a photo.
- **Passive, Double-Time:** the first skill each fight is free.
- **Evolutions:** Shuffle Kid → Tempo Dancer → 174 BPM Phantom.

### Wall of Sound (Hardstyle). Unlockable.
Built like a speaker stack and carries a shield shaped like one. Stands at the front of the barrier so smaller people can see.
- **Passive, Kick Drum:** starts every fight with Guard.
- **Evolutions:** Barricade Kid → Wall of Sound → Hardstyle Titan.

## 5. Your Lost Crew

The group chat is called **"NOCTURNE OR BUST 🌙"**. The emoji was Juno's idea. They're never met in person during a run. They exist only in **late texts**, **voice notes**, and **Lost Crew Sightings**:

- **Juno:** the planner. Laminated schedule, three portable chargers, all dead by now. Texts in full sentences with punctuation.
- **Paz:** the dancer. Gets lost on purpose. Voice notes are mostly crowd noise and laughing.
- **Big Remy:** gentle giant and totem-carrier. Lost the totem. Very upset about the totem.
- **Tilly:** first festival. Wide-eyed. Keeps finding small miracles and texting photos that never load.

## 6. Recurring Characters

### Glimmerwick, the Totem Wizard
A tall festival totem: a pole topped with a pool noodle, a disco ball and a cardboard wizard face. Someone, maybe Big Remy, left it standing in the dirt. It speaks at **Totem Pole Shrines**. Early in the night it's chatty and helpful. Deeper in, it gets **cryptic**. By Afterhours it speaks mostly in rhythm and riddles, as though it is the night talking.

> Early: "Hey! Lost? Everyone's lost. That's what festivals are for. Leave something, take something."
> Late: "the bpm of the stars is one hundred twenty eight. you are on the one. you have always been on the one."

### Halcyon Ray (never seen)
Your favourite DJ. They don't post, they don't do interviews, and they always end on a song nobody can Shazam. Every rumour of THE SET leads back to them.

### The Food Truck Folks
Each truck has a person with a name and a tiny philosophy, like **"Nana Bao"** or **"Taco Luis"**. They replace the old merchant: food, hydration, gear and remix tutorials.

## 7. Regions (The Night's Map)

The night warps as you go deeper. The visuals change in stages: an ordinary night sky, then a **disco-ball moon**, then **melting stages**, then **glitching Drained**.

| # | Region | Look | Feel | DJ Boss |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Parking Lot Pregame** | Car roofs, pop-up tents, string lights, tailgates | Excitement, normal night | **MC Megaphone** |
| 2 | **Entry Gates** | Chain-link, wristband scanners, archway banners | Lines, anticipation, first strangeness | **MXRROR** |
| 3 | **Forest Stage** | Trees wrapped in fairy lights, lanterns, hammocks | Dreamy; the moon becomes a disco ball | **Kaleido** |
| 4 | **Silent Disco** | Headphone dome, three channels of coloured light | Eerie hush; you hear everyone else's song | **DJ Nullpointer** |
| 5 | **Bass Tent** | Striped big top, speaker stacks, fog | Physical, loud; the stage rigs begin to melt | **Baron Von Bassment** |
| 6 | **Ferris Wheel** | Giant wheel, carnival lights, balloons | Vertigo, nostalgia | **Ringmaster Revolve** |
| 7 | **Main Stage 3AM** | Massive LED rig, lasers, pyro, crowd silhouettes | Peak night; the Drained glitch | **Lady Subsonic** |
| ★ | **Sunrise Set** | An empty stage at the far edge, the sky just turning | Hope; THE SET should be starting | |
| ∞ | **Afterhours** | Every stage again, looped and wrong | The night refuses to end | Remixed bosses |

### Node Types
- **Battle / Elite:** Drained and worse.
- **Water Station** (rest): refill Hydration, rest, or practise a skill.
- **Food Truck** (shop): food, hydration, skills, gear, remixes.
- **Lost Crew Sighting** (event): someone saw someone who looked like your friend.
- **Lost & Found** (treasure): a bin of other people's stuff.
- **Totem Pole Shrine:** Glimmerwick's offers.
- **DJ Boss:** the guardian of each stage.

## 8. The DJ Bosses

Each boss is a DJ who got lost in their own set. When you beat one, they wake up a little and leave **last words**.

1. **MC Megaphone** (Parking Lot). A hype man who never stopped hyping. Phase 2: **HYPE TRAIN** (Hype stacks).
   *Last words:* "I was only supposed to warm up the crowd. Nobody told me when to stop."
2. **MXRROR** (Entry Gates). A chrome-faced DJ in a mirrored helmet. **Remixes your last skill back at you.**
   *Last words:* "Every set I played was someone else's. You were a good song, though."
3. **Kaleido** (Forest Stage). A prism-headed visual artist. **You see double:** single hits can strike the reflection instead.
   *Last words:* "There were always two of me. Only one of us was sad."
4. **DJ Nullpointer** (Silent Disco). A glitchy live-coder with a CRT head. **Scrambles the turn order.**
   *Last words:* "undefined is not a crowd. ...thanks for dancing anyway."
5. **Baron Von Bassment** (Bass Tent). A velvet-caped speaker aristocrat. **His attacks soak up your Vibe.**
   *Last words:* "I kept every bassline for myself. It got so quiet down there."
6. **Ringmaster Revolve** (Ferris Wheel). A carnival conductor in a striped coat. Phase 2: **SPIN CYCLE**.
   *Last words:* "Round and round. I forgot the ride is supposed to end so you can get back on."
7. **Lady Subsonic** (Main Stage 3AM). The headliner before the headliner. **Phase 2 is THE DROP**, a massive hit the moment she turns it up.
   *Last words:* "The sun's coming. Halcyon always plays it right before. Go. Go!"

## 9. Elites

- **Head of Security Gord:** a giant in a hi-vis vest, built like a wall. Strict, fair, oddly sweet.
- **Porta-Potty Mimic:** you knew this was coming. Absolutely no one is surprised.
- **Pyro Tech:** a stage-effects tech wired into the flame cannons. Strictly professional pyro.
- **Flailing Fella:** an inflatable tube man who gained a will of his own.

## 10. The Drained (Common Enemies)

Every Drained used to be a raver having the best night of their life. They're grey, slow and hungry for vibe. **They're never gory.** When beaten, they blink, smile and wander off to find water.

- **Parking Lot Pregame:** Glitter Gloop, Tailgate Drained
- **Entry Gates:** Wristband Wraith, Gate Drone
- **Forest Stage:** Flower Crown Drained, Moss Bouncer
- **Silent Disco:** Static Shusher, Headphone Ghoul
- **Bass Tent:** Fog Machine Phantom, Subwoofer Stack
- **Ferris Wheel:** Balloon Drained, Neon Moth
- **Main Stage 3AM:** Barricade Drained, Camera Drone

## 14. Writing Guide

- Write UI copy in **festival language**: CRASHED instead of DIED, WATER STATION instead of CAMPFIRE, GLOWSTICKS instead of GOLD.
- Glimmerwick's voice gets more abstract, lower-case and rhythmic with depth.
- Text messages carry timestamps, and the timestamps drift later the deeper you go.
- When in doubt, lean toward kindness. The night is weird, but it's on your side.
